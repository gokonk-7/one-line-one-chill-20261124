One line of horror for make.fun's "One Line, One Chill" bounty: a single
sentence of under 200 characters that has to frighten a reader in one
breath. A judge model reads the lines two at a time and keeps the scarier
one, weighing how frightening it is first, then how vivid, then how
original, then how well the sentence is built.

> The dentist went quiet, held up my X-ray, and pointed to the second row of teeth growing in behind mine — all of them smiling.

Eight candidate lines were drafted and tested in `variants.md`; the
dentist's X-ray won on all four criteria — body horror with intent, a
concrete image, an angle no mirror or ghost story uses, and a three-beat
sentence landing on the kicker.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints the entry line and nothing else. It asserts the line is a single
sentence under 200 characters before printing.
